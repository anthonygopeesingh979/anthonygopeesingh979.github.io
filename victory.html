Victory

You summon the fury of raging tides.

A wall of water crashes through the cavern.

The fire troll staggers — its flames sputter and shrink.

Steam rises from its skin like smoke from cooling iron.

It roars one last time… then falls to its knees.

The molten glow in its eyes dims to ash.

With a final hiss, the fire inside it dies.

Silence returns.

You are victorious.
